# 🐢 Turtle Trigger

## Take control of a fearless turtle and dive into an action-packed adventure filled with danger, strategy, and humor.

In **Turtle Trigger**, your goal is to make it through challenging obstacle courses, overcome enemies, and face the final boss - a mighty dragon.

**Can you make it to the end and claim victory?**

---

### 🎮 Gameplay Features

#### 🧩 Challenging Levels

Navigate through exciting stages full of traps and enemies.

#### 🐲 Epic Boss Battles

Test your reflexes and combat skills in intense showdowns.

#### 🔫 Weapon Variety

Switch between weapons on the fly and find the best way to crush your foes.

#### 🎮 Responsive Controls

* **Shoot with the left mouse button**
* Move with **Arrow Keys** or **WASD**
* Jump with **Spacebar**
* Switch weapons with **E** and **Q**


#### 🎨 Stylish Visuals

Vibrant graphics and fluid animations inspired by classic platform games.

---

### 🐉 Your Mission

Overcome every obstacle, master every stage, and defeat the dragon to prove that even a turtle can be legendary!
